⚡ Executive Summary & Gameplay Philosophy

Smooth Snake's core appeal lies in its continuous steering math, physics-based arc-length body trailing, sublinear growth curve, and tight corridor navigation against hazards. This specification builds upon those foundations by introducing high-skill spatial hazards and high-satisfaction powers that scale naturally with combo multipliers.

Core Design Rule: Every proposed hazard must maintain threadable clearance guarantees via flood-fill grid validation (as defined in js/spikes.js), and every power must synergize with steering momentum rather than replacing it.

👾 Proposed New Enemies & Hazards

New hazards test spatial awareness, reaction timing, and momentum control.

Hazard #1

Pulse Sentry (Telegraphed Laser)

Stationary crystal beacon that charges up over 2.0s with a visible laser sightline before firing a high-speed lethal laser beam across the corridor.

  • Charge Warning 2.0 sec
  • Beam Duration 0.6 sec
  • Cooldown 3.5 sec
  • Dodge Tactic Boost past laser path during charge
Hazard #2

Gravity Well (Singularity Void)

A swirling vortex anomaly that exerts radial gravitational acceleration on the snake's head and body segments, pulling nearby food into orbit.

  • Pull Radius 180 px
  • Max Pull Force 140 px/s²
  • Physics Model F = G / (dist + r)²
  • Interaction Slingshot steering or boost out
Hazard #3

Sawblade Cutter (Perimeter Hazard)

A high-speed spinning saw blade that glides back and forth along arena walls. If hit, it trims 25% of your tail length (converting segments into bonus pellets) instead of dealing heart damage!

  • Speed 160 px/s
  • Patrol Path Outer perimeter
  • Tail Trim Effect -25% segments → +Points
  • Risk / Reward Trade length for speed & space
Hazard #4

Shadow Mimic (Phantom Trail)

An ethereal ghost snake that replays your exact position and heading from 1.8 seconds ago! You must avoid intersecting your own ghostly past shadow.

  • Time Offset 1.8 sec delay
  • Ring Buffer Size 120 history samples
  • Collision Head vs Shadow segments
  • Tactical Challenge No tight 180° loopbacks!

🔮 Proposed New Power-Up Abilities

Special pickups that spawn periodically to grant tactical advantages.

Power #1

Phase Shift (Ghost Form)

Renders snake head and body completely phase-shifted for 3.5 seconds. Pass straight through spikes, wall margins, and your own tail with zero damage!

  • Duration 3.5 sec
  • Visuals Cyan translucency + particle aura
  • Invulnerability Spikes + Walls + Self
Power #2

Magnet Core (Attraction Field)

Generates an intense magnetic field pulling all food pellets and power-ups directly into the head from across a 220px radius.

  • Duration 6.0 sec
  • Attraction Speed 250 px/s
  • Combo Synergy Extremely easy combo stacking
Power #3

Chrono Dilator (Slow-Motion)

Slows down world physics and enemy movements by 65% while keeping snake turn rate and speed at 100%. Precision navigation made effortless.

  • Time Scale 0.35x world speed
  • Duration 4.5 sec real-time
  • Audio Effect Pitch-shifted slow-mo sound
Power #4

Plasma Tail Cannon

Converts tail length energy into high-speed energy bolts fired straight out from the head every time boost is tapped, shattering static spikes!

  • Shots 3 Plasma Bolts
  • Spike Impact Destroys static spikes
  • Cost -1 segment per shot
